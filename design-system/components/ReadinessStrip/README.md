# ReadinessStrip

The annunciator strip, the system's one signature element: like a cockpit annunciator panel, every requirement is a small cell lit in its status colour, so a person's or a department's readiness reads at a glance.

- **Three forms.** `ReadinessStrip`: compact cells grouped as training, documents and authorisations, with `ReadinessSummary` beside it ("3/6 valid, 1 due, 2 missing"). `AnnunciatorPanel`: labelled 48px cells with a code and a note, opening a requirements section. The department row on the dashboard: one cell per person, lit by their worst requirement.
- **Colours follow compliance, never the other way round.** Red is anything that blocks compliance: missing, expired, overdue, unverified, booked but not done, or a gap. Amber is valid but expiring within 60 days. An outlined cell is not yet assessed; grey is not applicable. Cells come from the same rules as `StatusBadge`, so a Non-Compliant person always shows red.
- **Never colour alone.** Red cells are hatched, every cell names its requirement and state on hover and to screen readers, and the summary states the counts in words. Show `ReadinessLegend` wherever the strip is new to the reader (panels, the dashboard).
- **Placement.** Under the name in a record header, as its own column in a list of people, as the opener of a requirements section, and per department on the dashboard. Not inside requirement table rows: those carry their own `StatusBadge` (`width="column"`).
- **Interaction.** Pass `onSelect` to make panel cells buttons that scroll to their requirement row; a dashboard cell opens the person. Any cell that is a button is at least `target-min` (24×24px) with a visible focus ring; the compact strip cells are never buttons.
- **`ReadinessCellButton`** is one clickable cell on its own (the dashboard's department rows, a cell per person that opens their profile): `state`, `label` (required: who or what, then the state, "Maria Konstantinou: Expiring soon"; it is the name and the tooltip) and `onSelect`. 24px square in the compact strip's colour, a slight lift on hover, the shared focus ring.
- Build `groups` with `getPersonReadinessGroups` from `lib/readinessStrip.ts`. API: `ReadinessStrip` (`groups`, `size`), `ReadinessSummary`, `AnnunciatorPanel` (`groups`, optional `onSelect`), `ReadinessCellButton` (`state`, `label`, `onSelect`), `ReadinessLegend`.
